Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is the HYM Annual Fundraiser? +

It's our one night each year to gather friends, families, and partners — to share stories from the year, celebrate the students God is moving in, and raise the support that makes the year ahead possible.

How do I reserve a seat or sponsor a table? +

Use the “Reserve your seat” button to open our Givebutter page, where you can claim individual seats or sponsor a full table. Sponsorships are a wonderful way to bring guests who are new to HYM.

I can't attend — can I still give? +

Absolutely. Every gift counts whether or not you're in the room. Use the giving page above to contribute any amount toward this year's goal.

Who can I contact with questions? +

Email us anytime at info@heritageyouthmissions.org and a member of the HYM team will be glad to help.
