01 · The point

Stop rebuilding the missions budget from zero.

The long-term vision is a permanent mission fund: a dependable base that can help cover relief work, travel, supplies, service projects, and ministry partnerships year after year.

That matters because needs do not wait for a fundraising calendar. A stronger fund gives HYM the ability to prepare young people, respond when communities need help, and support mission work with greater stability.

The evening is also part of the formation itself. Students see adults, families, churches, and businesses put their resources behind service—and they learn to give, work, and take responsibility alongside them.

What the number means: $179,272 is the result of the latest published foreign-missions fundraiser campaign. It is not presented here as HYM’s lifetime giving total or as a live 2026 campaign counter.

How one night carries forward

Gather. Grow. Go serve.

01

Gather the community

Families, churches, businesses, and friends come together to hear the mission story and take ownership of the work ahead.

02

Grow the mission fund

Gifts create a stronger base for future service instead of forcing the organization to start over each time a trip or urgent need appears.

03

Send prepared young people

The fund helps turn training and willingness into real action: travel, materials, relief response, partner support, and hands-on work.
