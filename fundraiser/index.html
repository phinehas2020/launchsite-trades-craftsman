Why we're doing this

Saying no isn't an option.

For ten years, our Texas church has given over a million dollars to serve communities around the world — funded one year at a time. Then an unexpected expense nearly drained the fund. So instead of scraping a budget together every year, we built something permanent.

$220,000+ Raised our very first night — the screen crossed $100K and just kept climbing.
$550,000+ We haven't spent a single penny of it. Local business owners invested it instead — and it's grown ever since.
$5,000,000 The goal: a seed fund we only invest and never spend — a portion grows it, the rest funds missions for good.

And it's not just the grown-ups giving — our young people alone have raised over $100,000, at $350 a year each.

Year one: $0 → $550,000. What will next year look like?

Good to know

Questions, answered.

What is the HYM Annual Fundraiser? +

It's our one grand night each year to gather as a community — to share the story of what God is doing through Heritage Youth Missions, celebrate together, and grow the mission fund that fuels the work ahead.

What does it cost to attend? +

Nothing — the evening is free and open to everyone. We simply ask that you come ready to give. Every gift goes directly into the mission fund.

How do I RSVP? +

Use the “Save your spot” button to let us know you're coming, so we can plan seating and food. Bring your family and friends — there's room at the table.

I can't attend — can I still give? +

Absolutely. Every gift counts whether or not you're in the room. Use the giving page above to contribute any amount toward the mission fund.

Can my business sponsor the event? +

Yes — business sponsors are a big part of the night, and we recognize our sponsors in front of the whole room. Email us and we'll share the details.

Who can I contact with questions? +

Email us anytime at info@heritageyouthmissions.com and a member of the HYM team will be glad to help.
