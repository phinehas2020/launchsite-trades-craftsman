Verified service project · Hunt, Texas

Ready hands.
Steady hope.

After flooding devastated families near Hunt, HYM students, leaders, and volunteers showed up for cleanup, supply support, meals, prayer, and practical care.

01 · The response

Compassion became physical work.

Heritage Youth Missions mobilized quickly after the flood, working alongside local churches and recovery partners to help neighbors facing loss.

Teams removed debris, helped clean homes, distributed supplies, served meals, prayed with families, and stayed useful while the longer work of recovery continued.

This is the kind of formation HYM exists for: young people learning that faithful service can look like a shovel, a shared meal, a listening ear, and the willingness to carry someone else’s burden.

How the team served

Immediate relief with long-term care in view.

01

Coordinate locally

HYM leaders worked with local church partners and recovery teams so volunteers could go where help was genuinely needed.

02

Do the hard work

Students and adults joined cleanup crews, moved debris, distributed supplies, supported meals, and met practical needs on the ground.

03

Care for people

Prayer, encouragement, and a steady presence mattered alongside the physical labor, especially after the first rush of attention faded.

Why it matters

Service forms the people who serve.

Hunt, Texas Flood Relief showed students that meaningful mission work is often ordinary, physical, and unseen. Faith becomes tangible through small acts done faithfully.
